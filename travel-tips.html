Things to Carry

  • Comfortable clothes and shoes
  • Water bottle
  • Phone and charger
  • Identity proof
  • Basic medicines
  • First-aid kit

Before You Travel

  1. Choose your destination.
  2. Check the weather forecast.
  3. Book your transport and hotel.
  4. Pack your bags.
  5. Keep your documents safe.
  6. Enjoy your trip!

Important Travel Tips

  • Respect local culture and traditions.
  • Keep public places clean.
  • Save emergency contact numbers.
  • Follow local safety advice.

Travel safely and create beautiful memories!
